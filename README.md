Section links and IDs are maintained in `research/index.html`; `assets/research-nav.js` highlights the overview's current section. Detail pages each cover one topic and link back to the overview. `research/notes/` redirects older hash links to these pages.

The red header uses white navigation text and a white current-page underline. Research and the header share `--site-width: clamp(1168px, 90vw, 1480px)`: the research body stays 864 px at a 1280 px viewport and grows to 1176 px on large displays, alongside the contents sidebar. Narrow screens shrink naturally and retain the existing single-column layout. Home, Personal Statement, and CV keep their reading widths. Above 1600 px, the three standalone research figures can grow to 920 px and RCD uses a smaller optical offset.

All public images and videos are in `pictures/`, including `home/`, `research/`, `cv/`, and `equations/`.
