About me

I’m Jingkun Jiang, a master’s student in Materials Science and Engineering at Hunan University, advised by Prof. Yang Xia. I work on scientific computing and high-performance computing for physical simulations.

I develop numerical solvers and parallel implementations to study crystal growth and microstructure evolution, and build tools that connect simulated fields with grain statistics. This work spans finite-difference and spectral methods, GPU computing, and distributed FFTs. Under Prof. Yang Xia’s supervision, I led the design and implementation of sp-DBA, an adaptive framework for transform-domain solvers, from method development and validation through manuscript preparation.
