About me

I’m a master’s student in Materials Science and Engineering at Hunan University, advised by Prof. Yang Xia. I work on numerical methods for materials simulation, with a focus on adaptive computation and parallel solvers.

I led the design and implementation of sp-DBA with my advisor, Prof. Yang Xia. The framework makes blockwise update decisions in Fourier space and aligns them with GPU execution. I developed its shared core and applications, ran the simulations and analysis, and wrote the first manuscript draft. I also build phase-field solvers and tools for extracting grain statistics.

Research interests

  • Scientific Computing
  • Phase-Field Modeling
  • High-Performance Computing (HPC)
  • Adaptive Computing
