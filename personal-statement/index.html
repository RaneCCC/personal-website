Personal Statement

My research began with building solvers for materials models. I implemented phase-field and phase-field-crystal methods, then developed parallel versions with OpenMP, MPI, and CUDA. I led the design and implementation of sp-DBA with my advisor, Prof. Yang Xia, bringing blockwise adaptivity to Fourier-domain computation. I developed the framework and its applications, ran the simulations and analysis, and wrote the first manuscript draft.

For a PhD, I want to study microstructure evolution and develop numerical methods that make larger simulations practical. I am interested in how adaptive computation can work with existing solvers and parallel hardware, and how to check its effects on the physical results. My experience with distributed FFTs and grain analysis gives me a starting point for these questions.
