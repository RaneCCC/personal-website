Correlation kernels and lattice order

I compared correlation kernels that favor different lattice spacings and symmetries. These two-dimensional tests include hexagonal and square ordering, along with BCC, FCC and simple-cubic lattice representations. Real-space density, Fourier-space views and spectral line cuts let me inspect how each kernel shapes the emerging pattern.
