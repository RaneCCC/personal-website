Distributed FFTs

I decomposed the PFC field into slabs, with each GPU performing local cuFFT transforms. Packing, MPI redistribution and unpacking move the data needed for the other transform direction. I integrated the adaptive update between the forward and inverse transforms and measured its cost within the complete distributed solver.

Block-state exchange

At slab boundaries, neighboring MPI ranks exchange block-state halos so local activation decisions include adjacent blocks on the other rank.

Strong and weak scaling

I compared Regular and sp-DBA PFC workflows on one to eight NVIDIA A800 GPUs. In weak scaling, sp-DBA reduced per-GPU update time by about 2–3×. The full-workflow breakdown shows how distributed transforms and communication limit the end-to-end gain.

Tests used 10,000 PFC steps on A800-SXM4 GPUs: a fixed 4096² grid for strong scaling, and 2048²–5760² grids with approximately constant area per GPU for weak scaling. Timings average three runs after warm-up; error bars show ±1 standard deviation. Full benchmark setup: Supplementary Information, Section S8 →
