Distributed FFTs

For larger PFC fields, I implemented a slab-decomposed workflow combining local cuFFT transforms with packing, MPI redistribution, and unpacking. This separates the local arithmetic from the data movement needed for a global transform. The workflow below retains the full FFT/IFFT and redistribution stages.

For sp-DBA, I exchanged block-state metadata between neighboring ranks so activation decisions remain coordinated at slab boundaries. These small halos supplement the full FFT redistribution. In one-to-eight A800 GPU tests, sp-DBA reduced the per-GPU update time by about 2–3× relative to Regular under weak scaling. Distributed transforms and communication still dominated overall runtime.
