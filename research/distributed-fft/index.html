Distributed FFTs

I decomposed the PFC field into slabs, with each GPU performing local cuFFT transforms. Packing, MPI redistribution and unpacking move the data needed for the other transform direction. I integrated the adaptive update between the forward and inverse transforms and measured its cost within the complete distributed solver.

At slab boundaries, neighboring MPI ranks exchange block-state halos so local activation decisions include adjacent blocks on the other rank. In weak-scaling tests on one to eight NVIDIA A800 GPUs, sp-DBA reduced per-GPU update time by about 2–3× relative to Regular. Timing the full workflow showed that distributed transforms and communication remained the main overall cost.
